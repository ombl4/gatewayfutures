What each latency means
End-of-turn detectionCaller stopped speaking → the turn detector decided the caller was done. Before this, the agent cannot start thinking.TranscriptionEnd of turn → the final transcript arrived from speech recognition.LLM first tokenTranscript sent to the language model → first token of the reply (time to first token, TTFT).TTS first byteFirst sentence sent to text-to-speech → first audio byte back (time to first byte, TTFB).PlaybackFirst audio byte → it is actually played into the room.Reported end-to-endThe agent's own sum for the turn (end of turn → audio playing), from its clock.HeardMeasured from the recording: caller's last word → agent's first sound. What a real caller waits.UnaccountedHeard minus the reported stages: network, audio buffering and VAD look-back that the agent cannot see.

The stages come from the agent's own metrics (LiveKit Agents: EOU, STT, LLM and TTS events), so they are what the pipeline saw. "Heard" is measured on the stereo recording and is the number to optimise; the stages say which part of the agent to look at. The greeting has no wait because nobody spoke before it.
